## 1. What Is Orbys360

Orbys360 is an AI-first GCC advisory platform — a curated directory and marketplace that connects Global Capability Centers seeking AI solutions with providers who build and sell AI agents and services.

**One-sentence description:**
> Orbys360 is the platform where GCC leaders discover, compare, and connect with vetted AI agents and service providers to accelerate their AI transformation.

**Category we're creating:** "AI-First GCC Platform" — the first platform purpose-built for the $64.6B GCC ecosystem that combines a curated marketplace, knowledge hub, and community in one place.

### The GCC Evolution

GCCs started as cost arbitrage centers. They evolved into delivery hubs, then innovation engines. The next phase — and the one Orbys360 is built for — is the **AI backbone of the enterprise**: where AI transformation happens, scales, and is governed.

| Phase | Focus | Era |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Cost center | Back-office support, labor arbitrage | 2000s |
| Delivery hub | Execution at scale, process maturity | 2010s |
| Innovation engine | Product ownership, IP creation | 2015–2024 |
| **AI backbone** | **Cognition, co-creation, AI-native ops** | **2025+** |

### Why Now

Three forces are converging:
1. **AI maturity** — Enterprise AI has moved from experimental to board-mandated. Every GCC has an AI transformation mandate.
2. **GCC evolution pressure** — 1,700+ GCCs are being asked to move beyond cost savings to value creation.
3. **Talent concentration** — 2.1M GCC professionals are the largest pool of enterprise operations talent globally, and they need AI enablement.

The first platform to own this intersection wins.

## 3. Who We Serve

### Persona 1: GCC Buyer

**Who they are:** Leaders at Global Capability Centers — VPs of Global Operations, Heads of Shared Services, Chief Transformation Officers, AI Transformation Leads. Typically at Fortune 500 companies with 5,000+ employees globally.

**What they need:**
- Discover AI agents that solve real GCC use cases (finance, HR, IT ops, supply chain)
- Compare solutions side-by-side with objective criteria
- Get peer validation — what are other GCCs doing?
- A structured path from fragmented AI pilots to enterprise-scale transformation

**Their pain:**
- Running 10+ AI pilots that never scale to production
- Drowning in vendor pitches with no way to validate enterprise-readiness
- No GCC-specific frameworks — every transformation is reinventing the wheel
- No visibility into what peers are doing successfully
- Board demanding an AI roadmap and they're starting from scratch

**Emotional state:** Stressed by transformation pressure, fearful of making wrong vendor choices, frustrated by lack of structured guidance.

**What they do on the platform:**
- Browse the agent directory (229 agents across 78 companies)
- Filter by function (9 categories), industry (13 verticals), and search
- View detailed agent profiles (use cases, integrations, expected outcomes)
- Compare up to 4 agents side-by-side
- Shortlist agents to their GCC dashboard
- Submit contact requests to connect with providers (admin-mediated)

### Persona 2: Provider

**Who they are:** AI solution companies, technology service providers, and startups that build AI agents and services for enterprise use cases. Roles: CEOs, Heads of Sales, BD Directors, Partnership Leads.

**What they need:**
- Access to qualified GCC buyers without cold outreach
- Enterprise credibility by being listed in a curated directory
- A way to showcase their AI agents with rich, structured profiles
- Shorter sales cycles through pre-positioned, discoverable solutions

**Their pain:**
- 12–18 month sales cycles with GCC buyers
- Every deal requires custom positioning — no reusable channel
- Difficulty proving enterprise readiness and production-grade capability
- Limited access to GCC decision-makers
- No structured demand visibility across the GCC lifecycle

**What they do on the platform:**
- Claim their existing company profile (78 companies pre-seeded) or submit a new one
- Manage company information (description, website, team)
- Submit and manage AI agent listings (with admin review)
- Respond to inbound interest from GCC buyers
- Invite team members to collaborate on the profile

### Persona 3: Admin (Internal)

**Who they are:** Orbys360 team members who moderate quality across the platform.

**What they do:**
- Review and approve/reject claim requests, new company submissions, company edits
- Review and approve/reject new agent submissions and agent edits
- Manage contact requests between GCC buyers and providers
- Monitor directory health (total agents, companies, claimed %, pending items)

## 9. Brand Voice

### Tone Principles

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Be direct and substantive | Don't be fluffy or hype-driven |
| Use GCC-native language confidently | Don't over-explain what a GCC is to a GCC audience |
| Show domain expertise | Don't sound like a generic AI company |
| Be opinionated when it matters | Don't hedge on everything |
| Speak to practitioners, not just executives | Don't be corporate jargon soup |

### Voice Characteristics

- **Knowledgeable** — We understand GCCs deeply. We speak the language of operating models, charters, governance, and transformation.
- **Direct** — We lead with the point. No throat-clearing, no excessive preamble.
- **Practical** — We're about outcomes, not theory. Frameworks that work, solutions that ship.
- **Confident but not arrogant** — We've earned the right to have a point of view, but we're building with the community, not lecturing at it.

### Language to Use

- "AI-first" (not "AI-powered" or "AI-enabled" — stronger positioning)
- "GCC" (not "captive" or "shared services center" — modern terminology)
- "Curated" (not "comprehensive" — quality over quantity)
- "Platform" (not "tool" or "product" — conveys ecosystem)
- "Enterprise-ready" (not "scalable" — more specific to our audience)
- "Transformation" (not "digital transformation" — broader, more modern)

### Language to Avoid

- "Revolutionize" / "disrupt" / "game-changing" — overused, empty
- "Synergy" / "leverage" / "paradigm" — corporate cliché
- "Best-in-class" / "world-class" — unsubstantiated superlatives
- "One-stop shop" — commodity framing
- "Cutting-edge" / "state-of-the-art" — says nothing specific

## 11. Monetization (Future)

Everything is **free** right now. The priority is building the directory, driving traffic, and getting companies to claim profiles.

**Planned revenue model (sequenced):**

1. **Phase 1 (now):** Free claiming, free browsing — maximize adoption
2. **Phase 2:** Freemium analytics for providers (who viewed your agents, search impressions)
3. **Phase 3:** Sponsored placement in directory (featured badges, top-of-results)
4. **Phase 4:** Lead generation fees (charge per qualified buyer connection)

Revisit monetization once the directory has 50+ claimed companies and measurable buyer traffic.
