1. **Respond to comments within 2 hours** during business hours
2. **Like every comment** on your posts

## Performance Tracking

### Metrics to Monitor

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Impressions** | Track growth week-over-week | Baseline in Week 1 |
| **Engagement Rate** | >2% | (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Impressions |
| **Comments** | >5 per post | Priority metric — comments > likes |
| **Profile Visits** | Track conversion to website | From LinkedIn analytics |
| **Waitlist Signups** | Track from Day 7 and Day 14 posts | Primary conversion metric |

### Optimization Notes

1. **If engagement is low:** Adjust posting time, test different hooks
2. **If comments are low:** End posts with more direct questions
3. **If promotional posts underperform:** Reduce promotional frequency, increase value content
4. **If weekends underperform:** Consider skipping Sat/Sun posts
