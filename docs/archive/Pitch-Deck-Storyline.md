# Orbys360 Pitch Deck Storyline

> **Purpose:** A 10-slide narrative for presenting Orbys360 — The AI-First GCC Platform
> **Type:** Generic pitch deck (not VC-specific)

---

## Slide 1: The Opening Hook

**Title:** The AI Core of the Enterprise

**Storyline:**
Open with the big idea — GCCs are at a pivotal moment. They're no longer just operational hubs; they're becoming the AI backbone of global enterprises. But there's a gap between where they are and where they need to be.

**Content:**
- Headline: "The Future of Global Capability Centers Is AI-First"
- Subheadline: "A platform to accelerate the AI-enablement of GCCs worldwide"
- Visual: Global map with GCC hotspots (India, Philippines, Poland, etc.) connected to enterprise headquarters

---

## Slide 2: The Market Opportunity

**Title:** A $64.6 Billion Ecosystem Ready for Transformation

**Storyline:**
Establish the scale and momentum of the GCC market. This isn't a niche — it's a massive, fast-growing ecosystem actively seeking AI enablement.

**Content:**
- **$64.6B** — Annual GCC revenue globally
- **1,700+** — GCCs operating in India alone
- **2.1M** — Professionals employed in GCCs
- **11% CAGR** — Market growth over 5 years
- **50-55%** — Share of world's GCCs hosted in India

**Key Message:** "The largest concentration of enterprise operations talent in the world is ready to become AI-enabled."

---

## Slide 3: The Problem — Enterprises & GCCs

**Title:** The AI Transformation Gap

**Storyline:**
GCCs are under pressure to become AI-enabled, but they're stuck. Fragmented pilots, no structured path, no peer benchmarks. Every GCC is reinventing the wheel.

**Content:**
- Fragmented AI pilots that never scale to production
- No GCC-specific frameworks or operating models
- Endless vendor noise with no way to validate enterprise readiness
- No visibility into what peers are doing successfully
- Slow progress from intent to impact

**Key Message:** "GCCs have the mandate to transform. What they lack is the playbook."

---

## Slide 4: The Problem — Service Providers

**Title:** The Access & Credibility Gap

**Storyline:**
On the other side, AI solution providers struggle to reach GCC buyers. Long sales cycles, custom project overhead, and difficulty proving enterprise readiness make the GCC market hard to crack.

**Content:**
- Limited access to GCC decision-makers
- 12-18 month sales cycles
- Every deal requires custom positioning
- Difficulty establishing enterprise credibility
- No structured demand visibility across GCC lifecycle phases

**Key Message:** "Providers have the solutions. What they lack is access and positioning."

---

## Slide 5: The Solution — Orbys360

**Title:** The AI-First GCC Platform

**Storyline:**
Introduce Orbys360 as the platform that bridges both gaps. A comprehensive ecosystem combining marketplace, knowledge, community, and intelligence — purpose-built for the GCC market.

**Content:**
- **For Enterprises & GCCs:** Curated frameworks, ready-to-deploy AI solutions, and a community of Fortune 500 peers
- **For Providers:** Direct access to GCC buyers, enterprise credibility, and structured demand visibility

**Visual:** Platform diagram showing the two-sided marketplace connecting enterprises and providers

**Key Message:** "One platform. Two audiences. One mission: Accelerate the AI-enablement of GCCs."

## Speaker Notes Summary

**Opening (Slides 1-2):** Set the stage. Make the audience feel the size and momentum of the opportunity.

**Problem (Slides 3-4):** Build tension. Show that both sides of the market are struggling — and the current solutions aren't working.

**Solution (Slides 5-6):** Release the tension. Introduce Orbys360 as the answer that bridges both gaps.

**Differentiation (Slide 7):** Build credibility. Show why we're uniquely positioned to win.

**Value (Slide 8):** Make it tangible. Translate features into outcomes that matter.

**Close (Slides 9-10):** Create momentum. Invite them to be part of something bigger than a product.
