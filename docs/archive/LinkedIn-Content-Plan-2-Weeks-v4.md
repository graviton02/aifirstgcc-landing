Carrying yesterday's workflows into an AI-first world is a recipe for irrelevance.

4. Data Flow Bottlenecks
Where do workflows stall waiting for information?
Agents can pull, synthesize, and route data in real-time.

5. Agentic Potential
Which workflows are prime candidates for autonomous AI agents?
Look for high volume, rule-based, and cross-system processes.
